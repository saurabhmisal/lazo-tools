# Lazo voice and language

Owner direction — 7 November 2026.

## Belief

Give people more room to think and an extension to help them act on that thinking. People set the direction, review the work and make the calls.

The Lazo headline stays exactly **You, but amplified!**

## How we write

- Lead with the job or outcome. Name the thing people can do.
- Use familiar verbs: think, find, write, plan, make, review, share.
- Keep marketing paragraphs to one or two short sentences. Aim for 25 words or fewer.
- Make each sentence add information. Remove repeated promises and decorative slogans.
- Prefer concrete nouns: notes, drafts, tasks, sources, prototypes, updates.
- Address people as “you”. Explain Lazo's part without taking away their agency.
- Keep each product distinct: Pencil connects context; Everyday organizes daily work; Power Chords gives agents reusable workflows.
- Use consistent actions: sign up, verify email, download; “Try the demo” always opens a labelled demo.

## Be precise

Current capabilities, planned work and illustrations must be distinct. Never imply mind-reading, automatic execution, guaranteed impact or production readiness. External actions need review and authorization. Keep necessary installation, privacy, cost and compatibility details accessible, even when they need more words.

Keep technical workflow instructions and third-party licence notices complete. Simplicity must not remove facts that help someone use the tool correctly.

## Typography

Commissioner was selected by the owner on 7 November 2026. Use locally hosted variable WOFF2 fonts, regular body and semibold headings. Keep the established colour system. Kalam remains for illustration notes, and code stays monospace. No claim is made that a font is exclusive to human-designed work or unused by generative AI.
